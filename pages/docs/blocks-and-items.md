# Blocks and items

## Work contract

:mc-work_contract: Hires an unemployed adult
villager. Right-click the villager with it; the contract is used up.

[[craft shapeless: paper ink_sac emerald emerald emerald -> work_contract]]

Shapeless: the ingredients can go anywhere in the grid.

## Wicker basket

:mc-wicker_basket: Contract and workstation
of the [carrier](workers/carrier.md). Holds 6 stacks and up to 3 routes. Right-click the
air with it to plan routes.

[[craft: sugar_cane stick sugar_cane / sugar_cane _ sugar_cane / sugar_cane sugar_cane sugar_cane -> wicker_basket]]

## Chopping block

:mc-chopping_block: Workstation of the [lumberjack](workers/lumberjack.md). Villagers walk around it
instead of climbing on it.

[[craft: _ any_axe _ / _ any_log _ -> chopping_block]]

## Woodcutter

:mc-woodcutter: The stonecutter for wood
and workstation of the [sawyer](workers/sawyer.md). Turns logs, stems and bamboo blocks
into planks, stairs, slabs, fences, doors, signs, ...; planks into stairs, slabs,
buttons and sticks.

[[craft: _ iron_ingot _ / any_planks any_planks any_planks -> woodcutter]]

## Carpenter's bench

:mc-carpenter_bench: A crafting table for wooden goods and workstation of the
[carpenter](workers/carpenter.md).

[[craft: any_wooden_slab any_wooden_slab any_wooden_slab / _ iron_ingot _ / any_log _ any_log -> carpenter_bench]]

## Stonecutter (vanilla)

:mc-stonecutter: Workstation of the [stone sawyer](workers/sawyer.md). Every stonecutter
opens the cutter menu of this mod, which looks and works like the vanilla one but
has the order box and the **i** button.

## Composter (vanilla)

:mc-composter: Workstation of the [farmer](workers/farmer.md). Right-click it with an empty hand to open
its overview. When it is full, the bone meal comes out first; with an item in your hand it
composts as usual.

## Blast furnace (vanilla)

:mc-blast_furnace: Workstation of the [smith](workers/smith.md). It opens as usual; the **i** button at
the top right of its screen opens the overview.
