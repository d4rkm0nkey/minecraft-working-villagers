# Planned features

Working Villagers is in an early beta, and there is a lot more to come. This page shows
what is planned. Nothing here is in the game yet.

!!! info "Plans, not promises"
    The order may change, and some ideas may turn out differently once they are tried in
    the game. Ideas and wishes are welcome in the
    [issues](https://github.com/d4rkm0nkey/minecraft-working-villagers/issues).

## For the workers you already have

- :mc-work_contract: **A contract for every profession.** Instead of one contract you craft,
  each profession gets its own: a lumberjack contract, a farmer contract, a pit digger
  contract, ... They cannot be crafted. You find them in chests of villages, mineshafts,
  pillager outposts and woodland mansions, and a villager hired with one only takes jobs of
  that profession.
- :mc-experience_bottle: **Experience levels.** Workers rise from novice to master by
  working instead of trading, and get faster or carry more on the way.

## Villages

- :mc-bell: **Buying a village.** Workers belong to a village you own, and the village
  has a treasury that pays their wages.
- :mc-writable_book: **Village book.** One book with every worker and what it is doing,
  the villagers of the village, its supplies and its treasury.
- :mc-clock: **Working without players nearby.** A village you own keeps its workplaces
  loaded as long as the treasury covers the wages, so the work goes on while you are
  away.

## Economy

- :mc-wheat: **Food and housing.** Workers who get food and a bed from the village need
  less wage, so farmers and fishers have a use beyond selling.
- :mc-emerald: **Income for your village**: taxes from the villagers and orders from
  neighbouring villages, each with a natural limit so the village does not print money.

## Job books

:mc-written_book: Every profession gets a book that explains it in the game: the
workstation, the setup and what its status messages mean. Villagers sell them now and
then, so you can learn the mod without leaving the game.

## Other mods

:mc-spyglass: Support for recipe viewers (JEI, EMI, REI) to show the woodcutter and
carpenter's bench recipes, and for Jade to show what a worker is doing when you look at
it.
