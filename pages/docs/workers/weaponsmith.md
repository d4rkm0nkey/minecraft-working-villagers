# Weaponsmith

Hired with a work contract, the weaponsmith works at a vanilla **grindstone**. It forges new
tools and weapons, so your workers no longer need tools from you, grinds two worn tools into
one and takes the enchantments off loot, for the experience.

!!! note
    Ordinary (not hired) villagers still become vanilla weaponsmiths at grindstones. A
    vanilla weaponsmith can be hired directly: it stays at its grindstone, keeps its trades
    and becomes the weaponsmith of this mod. The weaponsmith trades like a vanilla weaponsmith.

## Setting it up

1. Place a grindstone and open it: the [tabs](basics.md#the-overview) above its screen lead
   to the overview. Link an **input chest** with the material and an **output chest**.
2. Tab :mc-stone_sword: **Order**: click the product, set the **stock**, and below it what
   it does besides (< and >).
3. Give it its wage.

## Forging

It makes **axes, pickaxes, shovels, hoes, swords, spears, shears** and the **mace** after the
vanilla crafting recipes, of cobblestone, copper, iron, gold or diamond, with sticks.
Wooden tools are the [carpenter](carpenter.md)'s. It fetches material for up to 4 pieces at
once and keeps the stock in its output chests. With a [village chest](basics.md#village-chests)
as output chest your workers take their new tools from there by themselves:
ore from the [miner](miner.md), ingots from the [smith](smith.md), sticks from the
[sawyer](sawyer.md), tools from the weaponsmith.

## Grinding together

Like a grindstone in your hands: **two worn tools of the same kind** (two iron axes) become
one, with the durability of both plus 5%. It takes them from the village chests in reach,
where workers give back their [worn tools](basics.md#worn-tools), and from its input chests,
and brings the result back to the chest it came from. That saves ingots: the smith repairs
with material, the weaponsmith without. **Enchanted or renamed** tools it leaves alone, a
grindstone would take the enchantments off; those stay with the smith.

## Disenchanting

Everything enchanted in its **input chests** (loot of the [guards](guard.md), treasure of
the [fisher](fisher.md), enchanted books) it disenchants like a grindstone: all enchantments
but curses come off. The **experience** it fills into :mc-experience_bottle: bottles
o' enchanting, 7 per bottle, with glass bottles from its input chests or a village chest
(the [apothecary](apothecary.md) makes them). The [smith](smith.md#bottles-o-enchanting)
repairs with them. It only takes from its input chests, so you decide what it disenchants.

## What it does first

Grinding together and disenchanting come before the order. Below the stock you choose:
both, only grinding together, only disenchanting, or only the order.

The menu shows how many pieces it made or disenchanted today.
