# Fisher

Hired with a work contract, the fisher works at a vanilla **barrel**. It fishes at the water
near the barrel and brings the catch to a chest. Give it a **boat** and it rows out onto a
lake or the sea and fishes there.

!!! note
    Ordinary (not hired) villagers still become vanilla fishermen at barrels. A vanilla
    fisherman can be hired directly: it stays at its barrel, keeps its trades and becomes the
    fisher of this mod. The fisher trades like a vanilla fisherman.

## Setting it up

1. Place a barrel near the water: a pond, a river, a lake or the sea.
2. Open the barrel: the [tabs](basics.md#the-overview) above its screen lead to the
   overview. The barrel itself stays a normal barrel for you.
3. Link at least one **chest** (**+ Chest**).
4. Right-click the fisher with its wage (emeralds) and a **fishing rod**; for trips out on
   the water also a **boat**.

**Without a chest or a rod the fisher does not work.**

## From the shore

The fisher walks to the closest bank of water within **16 blocks** of the barrel (4 blocks
up or down; a pond of a few blocks is enough), casts and waits for a bite, **5 to 30
seconds** like a player. You see its bobber on the water with the line to the rod; when a
fish bites, the bobber dips, the fisher reels in and the catch flies out of the water to
it: mostly fish, sometimes junk, from the vanilla fishing loot. After **12 catches** it
brings them to the chest and goes again.

## In the boat

If it carries a boat and there is **open water** within **32 blocks** of the barrel (at least
7 × 7 blocks of water, two deep, with only water on the way there):

1. it puts the boat on the water at a bank of that water (also if a pond is closer to the
   barrel) and gets in,
2. rows out to the open water,
3. fishes there; on open water it also catches **treasure** (enchanted books, bows, name
   tags, saddles, ...), like a player,
4. rows back with its 12 catches, gets out, takes the boat with it and brings the catch to
   the chest.

It finishes the trip even after work: it never stays out on the water at night. Switch the
trips off in the overview, tab :mc-oak_boat: **Settings**: **Row out in a boat** On / Off.

## Good to know

- The rod wears out like a player's, one use per catch. A plain rod is used until it
  breaks; an **enchanted or renamed** rod the fisher gives back shortly before, into a
  village chest or the chest of the barrel (see [Worn tools](basics.md#worn-tools)), so you
  can repair it at an anvil, or the [smith](smith.md#bottles-o-enchanting) with bottles o'
  enchanting. A rod with **Lure** gets bites sooner, one with **Luck of the
  Sea** catches more treasure.
- Rods and boats it takes from a [village chest](basics.md#village-chests) too.
- It hands in everything it catches; only its rods and its boat it keeps.
