# Librarian

Hired with a work contract, the librarian works at a vanilla **lectern** and enchants at an
**enchanting table** nearby. It puts the experience your village collects to use: the
:mc-experience_bottle: bottles o' enchanting of the [guards](guard.md#experience) and the
[weaponsmith](weaponsmith.md#disenchanting) become enchanted tools, weapons, armor and
books.

!!! note
    Ordinary (not hired) villagers still become vanilla librarians at lecterns. A vanilla
    librarian can be hired directly: it stays at its lectern, keeps its trades (enchanted
    books!) and becomes the librarian of this mod. The librarian trades like a vanilla
    librarian.

## Setting it up

1. Place a lectern and an :mc-enchanting_table: **enchanting table** within 6 blocks of it.
   Bookshelves around the table, like for you, make higher levels possible.
2. **Right-click the lectern with an empty hand** (without a book on it): its overview
   opens. Link an **input chest** and an **output chest**.
3. Tab :mc-enchanted_book: **Settings**: the level, "highest" by default.
4. Give it its wage.

## Enchanting

It enchants everything enchantable in its **input chests**: tools, weapons, armor, books
(the [tanner](tanner.md) makes them). It works like you taking the best offer of the
table:

- **The level** is up to 30 with 15 bookshelves (two levels per bookshelf, at least 8 at a
  bare table), or lower if you set it at the lectern. The enchantments are chosen like at
  the table, also several at once.
- **The price** instead of your levels: one bottle o' enchanting per 3 levels (10 at level
  30) and 1 to 3 :mc-lapis_lazuli: lapis lazuli, like the three offers of the table.
  Bottles and lapis come from its input chests or a village chest in reach.

It takes up to 3 pieces at once, needs 3 seconds for each and brings them to its output
chest. With a [village chest](basics.md#village-chests) as output chest your workers take
the enchanted tools from there.

The settings tab shows how many bookshelves count, the level and what one piece costs.
The menu shows how many pieces it enchanted today.
