# Shepherd

Hired with a work contract, the shepherd works at a vanilla **loom**. It shears the sheep
around the loom, breeds them with wheat while there are fewer than you want, and brings
the wool to a chest.

!!! note
    Ordinary (not hired) villagers still become vanilla shepherds at looms. Only hired
    villagers become shepherds of this mod. A loom you have set up (linked chests) is kept
    for hired workers, and **Assign** makes room there.

## Setting it up

1. Place a loom next to your sheep pen.
2. Open the loom and click the button with the **shepherd's face** at the top right: it
   opens the overview.
3. Link at least one **chest** (**+ Chest**). A hired villager without a job finds the
   loom by itself; **Assign** picks a specific one.
4. Right-click the shepherd with its wage (emeralds), **shears** and some **wheat** for
   breeding.

**Without a chest or shears the shepherd does not work.**

## How it works

During work hours the shepherd:

1. shears every sheep with wool within **12 blocks** of the loom (4 blocks up or down),
   **1.5 seconds** per sheep,
2. breeds the sheep while there are fewer than the **herd size** set at the loom: it feeds
   two grown sheep a wheat each, and they get a lamb, like after a player fed them,
3. brings the wool to the nearest chest once there is nothing left to do (or its
   inventory is full).

It reaches **over a fence**: a pen needs no gate (villagers do not open fence gates).
Sheep it cannot get at, it leaves alone for half a minute.

The shears wear out like a player's, one use per sheep, and are used until they break
(shears count as cheap tools, see [Worn tools](basics.md#worn-tools)). It carries up to 2
shears and keeps up to **16 wheat**; it needs the wheat only for breeding.

The menu shows how much wool it sheared today.

## The herd size

Tab :mc-white_wool: **Settings** in the overview: **−** and **+** set up to how many sheep
(lambs included) the shepherd breeds, from **0** (no breeding) up to **32**. The default is
**8**. Below it the menu shows how many sheep there are around the loom now.

## Good to know

- Sheared sheep grow their wool back by eating grass, so give the pen grass.
- The wool keeps the color of the sheep. Dye the sheep yourself for colored wool.
- Sort the wool by color with hoppers, or let a [carrier](carrier.md) take it on.
- The loom works as usual for you; the shepherd does not use it.
