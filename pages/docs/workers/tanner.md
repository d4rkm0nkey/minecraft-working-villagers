# Tanner

Hired with a work contract, the tanner works at a vanilla **cauldron**. It makes leather out
of what other workers have no use for, rabbit hide and the :mc-rotten_flesh: rotten flesh
your [guards](guard.md) bring home, and makes leather goods of it: books, leather armor
in the color of your village, bundles, saddles.

!!! note
    Ordinary (not hired) villagers still become vanilla leatherworkers at cauldrons. A
    vanilla leatherworker can be hired directly: it stays at its cauldron, keeps its trades
    and becomes the tanner of this mod. The tanner trades like a vanilla leatherworker.

## Setting it up

1. Place a cauldron and **right-click it with an empty hand**: its overview opens.
2. Link an **input chest** with the material and an **output chest**.
3. Tab :mc-leather: **Order**: click the products (up to 8, made in
   [equal parts](basics.md#several-products)), set the **stock** and the **color**.
4. Give it its wage and a :mc-bucket: **bucket**, so it can fill the cauldron itself (water
   within 16 blocks; rain fills it too).

## Products

| Product | Material |
| --- | --- |
| :mc-leather: Leather | 4 :mc-rabbit_hide: rabbit hide, or 4 :mc-rotten_flesh: rotten flesh (tanned) |
| :mc-book: Book | 3 paper, 1 leather |
| :mc-leather_chestplate: Leather cap, tunic, pants, boots | 5, 8, 7, 4 leather, and a dye |
| :mc-leather_horse_armor: Leather horse armor | 7 leather, and a dye |
| :mc-bundle: Bundle | 1 string, 1 leather |
| :mc-saddle: Saddle | 3 leather, 1 iron ingot |

It uses leather from its input chest first, then makes it of rabbit hide (from the
[butcher](butcher.md)'s rabbits), then tans rotten flesh.

## Tanning

Up to 16 rotten flesh go into the **water** of the cauldron, which takes one level of
water. After five minutes (half a Minecraft day) they are leather: 4 rotten flesh, 1
leather. Meanwhile the tanner does its other work. Bubbles rise from the cauldron while
it tans, and the menu shows how long it takes. If you break the cauldron, what is in it
drops.

This is not in vanilla. It can be switched off in the [configuration](../configuration.md#tanner-tanner).

## Colors

Below the stock you pick a **color** (< and >). The tanner dyes all leather armor it makes
in that color, with the :mc-red_dye: dye from its input chest, one per piece.

Leather armor of **another color** in its input chest it washes out in the cauldron (one
level of water each, like you would) and dyes again, also without an order. So you can
change the color of the village at any time.

## Uniforms for the guards

[Guards](guard.md#armor) put on armor from a village chest. A guard in a **leather tunic**
wears its tabard in the color of the tunic: dye the tunics red and all your guards wear red.
Put the tanner's output chest next to the guards' village chest, or make it a village chest.

The menu shows how many of the order it made today.
